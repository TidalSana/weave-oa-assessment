    - Rationale: Average files changed per PR (log-scale prevents LOC gaming)

  Final Impact Score Formula:

  impactScore =
    prsMerged * 10 +                    // Base contribution
    reviewsGiven * 15 +                 // Helping others (highest value!)
    reviewDepth * 5 +                   // Quality of reviews
    crossFunctionalReach * 3 +          // Breadth of knowledge
    Math.min(avgFilesPerPR, 20) * 2     // Scope (capped)

  Key Philosophy:

  Your scoring values collaboration and quality over raw output—reviews are
  weighted 50% higher than PRs because they unblock teammates. This prevents the
   "solo hero" anti-pattern and rewards engineers who lift the whole team.
